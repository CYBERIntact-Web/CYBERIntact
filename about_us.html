A small consultancy.
Deliberately built.

We are a small group of operators with backgrounds in threat intelligence, adversary simulation, and incident response across critical infrastructure. We built CyberIntact because the firms we had worked with were either too large to be precise or too narrow to be useful.

Every engagement is run by the people who scoped it. The operator who takes your brief is the operator who does the work. We are privately held, take no outside investment, and cap our partner count at five. That is what lets us turn down work that does not fit.

The doctrine
  1. 01
    We take our time.

    Good security cannot be rushed. We take time to properly understand your environment before making any recommendations. Getting it right matters more than getting it done quickly.

  2. 02
    We keep things focused.

    When every alert demands attention, none of them get it. We reduce the noise so your team can focus on what genuinely requires their time and judgment.

  3. 03
    We only deliver what we can stand behind.

    We do not produce reports full of uncertainty. Every finding we deliver is verified, ranked by priority, and paired with clear actions so your team always knows what to do next.

  4. 04
    We stay small on purpose.

    The person you speak to is the person who does the work. No handoffs, no account managers, no junior staff learning on your time. We turn down work that is not the right fit. That is how we protect the quality of everything we take on.

If the fit is there, we will know it in thirty minutes.
